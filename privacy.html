Privacy Policy

Hair Every Month · Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short: We collect no data. Your photos and everything you enter stay on your iPhone. There is no account, no cloud, no tracking and no analytics.

What we collect

Nothing. Hair Every Month does not collect, sell or share any personal data. The app has no servers and never sends your data to us or anyone else. The only network activity is Apple's App Store handling purchases, and opening this policy or the Terms in your browser.

What stays on your iPhone

The following is stored only inside the app on your device:

  • Your progress photos (front hairline, top and crown)
  • Your treatment details, schedule and reminder times
  • Your answers to the onboarding questions, including which treatments you use (for example, minoxidil or finasteride)

Photos are never saved to your photo library. The items above are excluded from iCloud and device backups, so they are not copied anywhere. If you delete the app, they are permanently deleted. To keep your own copy of your photos, use Export all photos in Settings at any time.

A few app settings, such as whether the app lock is on and whether you've finished onboarding, are kept in standard iOS settings storage, which iOS may include in your device backup. These settings contain no photos and no personal or health information.

Camera

The app uses your camera only to take your progress photos. Photos are processed and stored on your device and are never uploaded.

Face ID, Touch ID and passcode

If you turn on the app lock, iOS handles the check. The app never receives or stores your face, fingerprint or passcode data.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications created on your iPhone. They are not sent through any server. The reminder text is kept generic ("Time for your routine.") so nothing private appears on your lock screen.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Tracking, analytics and advertising

None. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code that collects data.

Sharing your photos

Your photos leave your iPhone only if you choose to export or share them yourself.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Not medical advice

Hair Every Month is for tracking only and does not provide medical advice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
