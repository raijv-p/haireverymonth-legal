Hair Every Month Support

Need help? Email haireverymonth@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Where are my photos stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside the app. They are never uploaded, never saved to your photo library and not included in iCloud or device backups.

How do I keep a copy of my photos?

Open Settings → Export all photos. You can save them to Files, AirDrop them or share them anywhere. Exporting is always free.

I got a new iPhone. Where did my photos go?

Because photos are never backed up, they don't move to a new phone automatically. Export them from your old iPhone before switching.

How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Hair Every Month and tap Cancel Subscription.

Reminders aren't arriving.

Check that notifications are allowed in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Hair Every Month, and that reminders are switched on in the app's Settings. Reminders are free for your first month and part of Premium after that.

Is this medical advice?

No. Hair Every Month is for tracking only and does not provide medical advice.
